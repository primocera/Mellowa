# MW-V12-06 · Make Premium's recurring value unmistakable in product and content

- **Run after:** MW-V12-05
- **Primary risk removed:** The free sample is clear, but monthly willingness-to-pay remains weaker unless users can see why Mellowa becomes more useful across days and weeks.

## Prompt

Perform a product-content and UX pass focused on recurring value, not feature expansion.

Core promise to preserve: "When your day changes, reshape what's left — without starting over."

Product boundaries:
- General wellbeing, not medical care, therapy, crisis support or disease-specific nutrition.
- No weight-loss promise, calorie obsession, streak shame or restrictive dieting.
- Completed items stay completed; only open items change.
- The user can inspect, edit and remove learned preferences.

Required work:
1. Audit landing, pricing, signup, onboarding, free sample, today, adjust, weekly reflection, favourites, meal rhythm, shopping drafts, progress, settings and paywall.
2. Make the difference explicit:
   - Free sample proves one realistic day.
   - Premium continues the loop across changing days and weeks.
3. Show recurring value through real product behavior:
   - reuse what fit;
   - visible/editable preference learning;
   - leftovers and shopping drafts;
   - weekly carry-forward;
   - faster adjustment without restarting.
4. At the paywall, connect each paid capability to a user problem rather than listing abstract features.
5. Do not promise that AI "knows you", improves health outcomes or provides therapy.
6. Remove repeated explanatory copy where the UI can demonstrate the behavior.
7. Keep the current trial grammar and exact disclosures consistent on every surface.
8. Add meaningful empty, loading, success, undo and error states for the recurring loop where missing.
9. Ensure low-capacity experiences reduce demand rather than create additional tasks.
10. Keep the visual language calm, warm and mobile-first.

Evidence and tests:
- Add content contracts for sample vs trial vs Premium.
- Add rendered tests for no-card sample, 3-day trial, charge timing and medical boundaries.
- Add browser journeys proving completed items stay, undo works and preference learning is inspectable/removable.
- Report before -> after copy only for materially changed lines.

Do not add a social feed, gamification, streaks, calorie targets or unrelated wellness features.
